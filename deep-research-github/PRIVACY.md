# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated / 最后更新:2026-11-05

## English

**Summary:** Deep Research runs entirely on your own computer. It does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data, and has no analytics or telemetry.

### What the plugin does with data
- **Local files only.** Research plans, source records, search logs and reports are written to a folder on your computer (`Documents/DeepResearch/` by default, or the folder named by `DEEP_RESEARCH_HOME`). The plugin never uploads these files. You can delete them at any time.
- **Network requests.** When your AI agent asks it to, the plugin fetches web pages or PDFs from the addresses the agent chooses, and queries Europe PMC (`ebi.ac.uk`) using the search terms the agent chooses. Those sites receive the request like any web request (your IP address, the requested address or search terms, and the user agent `deep-research-mcp/0.1`) and are governed by their own privacy policies.
- **No accounts, no tracking.** The plugin does not require sign-in, does not use cookies of its own, and sends nothing to the plugin author.

### Data handled by other parties
The AI application you use (for example Codex or Claude) and its model provider process your prompts, the pages the agent reads and the reports it writes, under their own terms and privacy policies. This plugin does not control that processing. Do not put confidential or patient-identifiable information into research prompts unless your own policies and your AI provider's terms allow it.

### Children
The plugin is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

### Changes and contact
If this policy changes, the new version will be published in this repository with a new date. Questions: open an issue in this repository's Issues tab.

## 中文

**简述:** Deep Research 完全在你自己的电脑上运行。它不会收集、在任何服务器上保存、出售或分享你的个人数据,也没有统计分析或遥测。

### 插件如何处理数据
- **只写本地文件。** 研究计划、来源记录、检索日志和报告都写入你电脑上的文件夹(默认 `文档/DeepResearch/`,或 `DEEP_RESEARCH_HOME` 指定的文件夹)。插件不会上传这些文件,你可以随时删除。
- **网络请求。** 当你的 AI 代理提出要求时,插件会访问代理指定的网页或 PDF,并用代理选定的检索词查询 Europe PMC(`ebi.ac.uk`)。这些网站会像普通网络请求一样收到你的 IP 地址、请求的地址或检索词以及 User-Agent(`deep-research-mcp/0.1`),并受其各自隐私政策约束。
- **无账号、无追踪。** 插件不需要登录,不使用自己的 Cookie,也不向插件作者发送任何数据。

### 其他方处理的数据
你使用的 AI 应用(如 Codex、Claude)及其模型服务商,会按它们自己的条款和隐私政策处理你的提示词、代理读取的网页以及生成的报告,本插件无法控制。除非你所在机构的规定和 AI 服务商的条款允许,请不要在研究提示词中放入机密信息或可识别患者身份的信息。

### 未成年人
本插件不面向儿童,也不向任何人收集个人数据。

### 变更与联系
本政策如有变更,会在本仓库发布新版本并更新日期。如有疑问,请在本仓库的 Issues 页面提问。
